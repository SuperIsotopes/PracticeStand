How it works

The blue cursor waits on the next notes. Play them and they turn green, then the cursor moves on and the page scrolls with you to the final bar. In a chord, every note counts; you can roll it if you like.

Turn on Microphone to listen to an acoustic or digital piano in a quiet room, or MIDI keyboard for a USB keyboard (most precise). The keys below light up with what to play, and you can click them or use your computer keyboard: A–K for white keys, W E T Y U for black keys, Z/X to shift octaves.

Want a different piece? Choose Open score… and load any MusicXML file, for example one exported from MuseScore.
